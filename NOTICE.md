# NOTICE — Image Rights & Usage

BangladeshiVQA pairs original Bangla annotations with images gathered from three sources.
The **annotations** (questions, answers, type/level labels, splits) are released
by the dataset authors under **[CC BY-NC 4.0](LICENSE)**. The **images** are governed by
the terms below.

## Image sources

The 2,068 images come from:

- **Primary crowdsourcing — 1,170 images.** Photographed by the authors and local
  volunteers, peers, and family members in Bangladesh. Informed consent was obtained from
  identifiable subjects for **non-commercial academic research use**.
- **Instagram public posts — 705 images.** Curated from public posts via cultural hashtags,
  then heavily filtered (≈2.35% retained).
- **Bangladeshi news portals — 193 images.** Sourced from public news pages.

## Usage terms

- Images are provided for **non-commercial academic research only**.
- The dataset authors **do not claim copyright or ownership** over public-media images.
  Public images **remain subject to their original ownership**.
- Use of these third-party images is intended as **Fair Use** for research and
  non-commercial purposes.
- **No person-level tasks.** The dataset provides no names, identity labels, biometric
  annotations, or person-recognition tasks.

## Privacy processing

Before release, all images were processed to protect privacy:

- **EXIF/GPS metadata, usernames, captions, comments, and platform identifiers removed.**
  All 2,068 released images were verified with ExifTool to contain **no EXIF or GPS
  metadata**.
- All files were **renamed to `CS_xxxx` format**, making it impossible to recover the
  original filename or source username from the released files.
- Public images were **manually screened**; private, explicit, vulnerable, or sensitive
  scenes were removed. Culturally and religiously sensitive images were manually reviewed.
- Images containing minors were retained only for ordinary, non-sensitive activities; no
  task identifies, categorizes, or tracks minors.

See **[TAKEDOWN.md](TAKEDOWN.md)**. Requested images will be removed from future versions.
