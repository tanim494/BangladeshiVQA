# Takedown Policy & Request Form

BangladeshiVQA includes images sourced from primary crowdsourcing and public media. We
respect the rights of individuals and copyright holders. If you are a **depicted person,
the image owner, or a copyright holder**, you may request the removal of any image.

## How to request removal

Email **tanim494@gmail.com** with the subject line **"BangladeshiVQA Takedown Request"** and
include the information below. You can also open a GitHub issue using the template, but
**do not post personal or identifying details in a public issue** — email is preferred for
anything sensitive.

### Request details

- **Image ID(s):** the `CS_xxxx` identifier(s) (from the filename or annotations).
- **Your relationship to the image:** depicted individual / image owner / copyright holder / other.
- **Reason for the request:** e.g., privacy, copyright, consent withdrawal.
- **Verification (if applicable):** anything that helps us confirm your relationship to the
  image, to the extent you are comfortable sharing.
- **Contact email** for our reply.

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge your request upon receipt.
2. We review and verify it.
3. Confirmed images are **removed from the dataset**, including future released versions
   and, where we control them, the external mirrors (Hugging Face, Zenodo).
4. We confirm completion to you.

We aim to act on valid requests promptly. Removal of an image does not affect the
non-identifying aggregate statistics reported in the paper.
